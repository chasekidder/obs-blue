# Phase 5: branding (watermark, outro card, live title)

Date: 2026-11-24
Branch: `improve/5-branding` (base: `improve/4-holding`)
Switch: `[branding] enabled` (default `false`); options `watermark_text` ("Your logo here"), `watermark_corner` ("top-right"), `outro_text`, `outro_s` (8, at most 15); `[facebook] live_title_template` (default `"{group_name} Sale – {date:%b %-d}"`)

## Watermark

One text source, "Branding Watermark" (`text_ft2_source_v2`, DejaVu Sans Bold 44, white at 70 %, outline), placed in every configured camera scene (one input, one scene item per scene, on top), 40 px from the configured corner of the 1080x1920 canvas. Created over obs-websocket if missing; afterwards the host can change its text or replace it with an image in OBS. It is a placeholder for a logo.

## Outro card

- "Outro" scene: a dark color source and `outro_text` centered; no microphone.
- At the end of a live, when the end starts in `LIVE` (power button, a long press, `stream-pilot press --long`), the end flow first puts "Outro" on air and waits `outro_s` while the status key counts down (`OUTRO 8` … `OUTRO 1`), then sends StopStream. Not in `RECONNECTING` (nothing would air it).
- After OBS stopped, the scene that was on air before the outro is put back, so the next pre-roll (after a long press) starts from a camera scene; with `[holding]` on, the holding scene takes over as usual.
- The outro is part of the end flow's stop phase (not cut by the budget), bounded by `END_OUTRO_CAP_S` = 17 s; `outro_s` is clamped to 15 s. The stop phase's worst case (outro 17 + StopStream 15 + waiting for OBS 15.5 + `fb_end_wait_s` 5 = 52.5 s) plus the 10 s tail reserve stays inside the 70 s budget (asserted in `test_recording.py`).

## Live title

- The 2026 producer has no "Add title" interstitial any more (none appeared in the test-Group lives on 2026-11-24). Its "Add post details" section has a button "What's your live video about?" that opens a "Create post" composer with a "Title (optional)" textbox, a "Description" textbox and "Save". After saving, the section shows the title and the button reads "Edit Post Details". (Recorded on the stream PC, test Group.)
- Before every pre-roll starts (after the stream key was read), stream-pilot fills `live_title_template` (`{group_name}`: `[facebook] group_name`; `{date}`: now, strftime codes) and saves. A broken template is logged and skipped.
- Selectors (`POST_DETAILS_BUTTON`, `POST_TITLE_FIELD`, `POST_DETAILS_SAVE`) are in `fb/selectors.py`; `Producer.set_title` uses them; the setup fixture has the composer (`test_producer.py`).
- The existing interstitial fallback (an empty required title box gets "Live") is unchanged.

## Off

No watermark, outro or title (today's behavior: the power press goes straight to StopStream). The restore hides the watermark and outro items and, if "Outro" is on air (stream-pilot died during an outro), puts the main camera scene on air.

## Robustness

Every step is a bounded feature hook: a failing title never blocks READY (test), a hung outro is cut at its cap and the stream still stops (test).

## Rollback

No new states or state fields. An older image leaves the watermark and "Outro" in OBS (the watermark stays visible until hidden in OBS).
